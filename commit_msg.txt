fix: zero centre-of-mass of sampled coordinates to prevent global drift

Without this fix, EDMSampler.sample() could return structures with
alpha carbons hundreds of ångströms apart even when training RMSD
looked healthy. The root cause was a training/sampling mismatch: the
model was trained on zero-COM inputs, but sampling drew the initial
noise from an uncentred N(0, I) prior and returned the raw model
output r_denoised without recentering it. Training RMSD was computed
on aligned or already-centred structures, so it never exposed the
global positional drift.

Two changes fix this:
- Zero the COM of the initial noise before the first denoising step
  so the trajectory starts in the prior the model was trained on.
- Subtract the COM of r_denoised before returning it so callers
  always receive a zero-centred structure regardless of any residual
  drift accumulated during the ODE integration.

Add test_edm_sampler_output_com_is_zero to verify the guarantee: with
an identity denoiser the returned coordinates must have COM < 1e-5 at
every batch element and coordinate axis.
