fix: add missing sqrt in S_churn noise injection formula

EDM Algorithm 2 scales the injected noise by sqrt(t_hat² - sigma²),
not by (t_hat² - sigma²) directly. The missing square root caused the
noise amplitude to grow as sigma² rather than sigma, producing an
injection ~1700x too large at the first step with the default schedule
(sigma_data=16, sigma_max=160, S_churn=0.2) and collapsing every
sample immediately.

The bug was invisible when S_churn=0 because t_hat==c_T at that point,
making t_hat²-c_T²=0 regardless of the missing sqrt. Any positive
S_churn activated the wrong branch and destroyed the trajectory.
