refactor: lift noise injection out of MainTrunk

Previously, MainTrunk.forward() sampled random noise and added it to
r_gt to produce r_input internally. This meant the actual denoiser
input was generated inside the forward pass — invisible in the batch,
impossible to inspect or reproduce independently, and untestable in
isolation.

Introduce r_gt_noised as an explicit FeaturizedBatch field that holds
the noisy coordinates at the current diffusion step. Noise injection
is now performed in featurize_batch() during training and in
build_sampling_context() during inference. MainTrunk.forward() reads
batch.r_gt_noised directly as r_input; it no longer generates noise.

Fix a latent semantic bug in EDMSampler.denoise(): per-step noisy
coordinates were stored in r_gt, silently overwriting the ground-truth
field across sampling steps. The EDM loop now updates r_gt_noised
instead, keeping r_gt as the immutable clean reference throughout.

Correct a wrong einops pattern introduced in the moved noise code:
"1 -> b 1 1" fails because b appears only on the right side and einops
cannot resolve its size. The correct pattern is "b -> b 1 1", which
names the existing batch axis on both sides and appends two singleton
dimensions for broadcasting against [B, N_atom, 3].

Update all test fixtures that construct FeaturizedBatch directly
(test_batch_types, test_featurize, test_main_trunk, test_seq_ce_loss)
to supply r_gt_noised. Fix the identity and half-denoiser mocks in
test_sampling to echo batch.r_gt_noised rather than batch.r_gt, since
r_gt is now the all-zeros ground truth and no longer carries the
evolving trajectory.
