fix: correct noise injection, c_noise formula, and sampler clamp

Training forward pass was missing three pieces needed to match the
EDM/AF3 diffusion specification:

Noise injection: MainTrunk received r_gt but never perturbed it.
Now internally samples zero-CoM Gaussian noise and scales by t_hat
(r_input = r_gt + t_hat * ε), consistent with the sampling loop.
The variable was also renamed from r_input to r_gt at the call site
to make the distinction between clean and noised coordinates clear.

c_noise formula: was 0.25 * log(t̂/σ_data + ε), which is wrong.
The correct AF3 formula is 0.25 * log(t̂); σ_data is already
absorbed into t̂ through the definition t̂ = σ_data * σ, so
dividing by σ_data again double-corrects the scale.

Sampler t_hat clamp: the S_churn augmented noise level is now
clamped to σ_data * σ_max so the sampler never injects noise that
falls outside the training distribution.
